A note from June

Welcome home.

June Henry Homes began with something deeply personal: the belief that this season of life should feel like coming home.

I wanted to create a place where later-life living feels warm, joyful, and full of possibility—not restrictive or institutional. A place where you can slow down, leave the worries of home maintenance behind, and simply enjoy your days surrounded by comfort, beauty, and genuine care.

With more than a decade in luxury hospitality, sales, event design, and client care, I’ve learned that true luxury is about far more than beautiful surroundings. It’s about how you feel. To feel welcomed. Seen. Remembered. Safe. And truly at ease.

That belief is at the heart of everything I create at June Henry Homes.

“I imagine the kind of home I would want for my own family.”

I imagine the kind of home I would want for my own family—a place where mornings feel peaceful, meals bring people together, friendships grow naturally, and every detail is thoughtfully cared for.

For me, this is more than hospitality. It is a calling to create a place where people can exhale, feel at home, and rediscover the simple joy of everyday living.

Welcome to June Henry Homes—a place where comfort meets connection, luxury feels personal, and every guest is welcomed like family.

June Henry
